Proximity success overestimates usable aerial navigation: an episode can stop near the target yet end behind occlusion. **PERCH** replaces proximity-only success with viewpoint-conditioned **V-SR / V-SPL**, and composes three modules—occupancy-driven viewpoint affordance (**map to see**), VLM-verified candidate belief (**see to trust**), and a verifiable-reward viewpoint policy (**trust to stop**)—into a closed Perception–Evidence–Reinforcement chain. Deployed on an edge multirotor, PERCH stops at usable viewpoints and holds watch over real outdoor targets.
