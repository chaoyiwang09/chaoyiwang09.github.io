Edge UAVs must commit to the correct target under tight compute, time, and motion budgets—a wrong early commitment can forfeit the mission. **STARE** treats reliability as a resource acquired by spending: cascaded evidential introspection, a budget-constrained Lagrangian-PPO evidence allocator, and Learn-then-Test commit calibration give finite-sample, distribution-free control of error-commit risk. Across aerial datasets and frozen depth backbones, STARE raises end-to-end success while keeping selective-error estimates below calibrated targets; the same agent commits correctly on real outdoor flights and abstains when evidence stays thin.
